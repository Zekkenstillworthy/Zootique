How to scan a visitor ticket

  1. Ask the visitor to open their booking confirmation on their phone.
  2. Scan the QR code with your device camera or a USB barcode scanner.
  3. Paste the full scanned text into the field below, or type just the booking reference (e.g. BK-1001).
  4. Click Validate ticket and check the result.
  5. Allow entry only if a green Valid message appears.
If validation fails…
  • Invalid format — paste the complete QR text, not just part of it.
  • Booking not found — check the booking reference with the visitor.
  • Not paid — visitor must complete payment before entry.
  • Cancelled — contact a Zoo Admin; no entry.
  • Wrong zoo — ticket is for a different establishment.
  • Already used — ticket was scanned earlier; check with your manager.
