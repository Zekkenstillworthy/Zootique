For Zoo & Wildlife Park Owners

Register Your Establishment on Zootique

Scale your park operations, enable seamless visitor bookings, manage staff tasks, and reach thousands of guests nationwide with our dedicated multi-tenant platform.

1

Create Admin Account

Provide your establishment details, location, and account credentials for your primary administrator.

2

Upload Documents

Submit 4 regulatory documents: Business Permit, Wildlife/Zoo License, Proof of Address, and Representative ID.

3

Super Admin Verification

Our compliance team verifies your legitimacy and credentials. You receive an automated approval notification.

4

Go Live & Manage

Log in, configure your zones, animals, ticketing services, and start accepting visitor bookings immediately.

Required Verification Documents

Have digital copies (PDF, JPG, PNG) ready before starting:

  • Mayor's / Business Permit — Current municipal registration
  • Wildlife Exhibition / Zoo License — DENR or government wildlife authority accreditation
  • Proof of Establishment Address — Utility bill or lease agreement
  • Authorized Representative ID — Government-issued ID with photo
