About Us

A unified platform connecting visitors to nature while empowering wildlife parks and animal farms with modern management tools.

Zootique is built to keep bookings, services, and operations seamlessly organized across partner establishments. Visitors can discover curated wildlife parks and manage reservations, while zoo administrators coordinate daily activities, manage animals and events, and access direct Superadmin support.

For Visitors

Discover partner zoos and wildlife parks, book tickets and experiences, and leave reviews.

For Zoo & Farm Admins

Manage services, animals, bookings, events, and revenue from one dashboard.

For Multiple Locations

Each registered zoo and farm gets its own branded space on the platform.

Platform Support

Admins can reach Superadmin support directly for platform issues and feedback.
