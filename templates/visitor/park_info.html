Getting Here

Zootique Main Entrance

123 Wildlife Boulevard, East Capitol Drive, Metro City, 1001

By Car

Ample parking is available at the main entrance. Parking fee is ₱50 for cars and ₱20 for motorcycles per day.

By Public Transit

Take the City Bus Route B and alight at the Zoo Gate stop. The entrance is a 2-minute walk from the stop.

Park Rules & etiquette

To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all guests and our animals, please observe the following rules during your visit:

Do not feed the animals
Our animals have strict diets. Feeding them outside of designated feeding sessions is strictly prohibited.
No flash photography
Flash photography can startle or harm the animals, especially in indoor or nocturnal exhibits.
No pets allowed
For the safety of our animals and your pets, only certified service animals are permitted.
Keep the park clean
Please dispose of your trash in the designated recycling and waste bins located throughout the park.
