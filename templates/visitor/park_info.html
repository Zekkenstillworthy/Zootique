Accessibility & Guest Accommodations

We are dedicated to providing a comfortable, enjoyable wildlife experience for all guests and families.

Paved Visitor Paths
Main paths connecting all habitat zones and viewpoints are paved and step-free for wheelchairs and strollers.
Accessible Restrooms
Spacious accessible restrooms and baby care changing stations are situated at the park entrance and central hub.
Service Animals
Trained service animals on leash or harness are welcome in guest visitor areas with designated relief zones available.
Rest Areas & Seating
Shaded seating benches and hydration fountains are placed throughout every habitat trail for guest relaxation.
